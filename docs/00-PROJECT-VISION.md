# Project Vision

## What is The 0-1?

The 0-1 is a premium, interactive digital magazine and visual briefing focused initially on global artificial intelligence and important technology news.

It should feel like an immersive digital book, not a normal blog, plain newsletter, static PDF, or generic news-feed website.

## Initial audience

- Developers and engineers
- AI builders
- Startup founders and employees
- Product professionals
- Technical leaders
- Serious engineering students

## Initial editorial focus

- Global AI news: models, labs, agents, research, chips, safety, regulation, and adoption
- Developer tools: APIs, frameworks, cloud, databases, security, open source, coding tools
- Technology business: startups, funding, companies, products, acquisitions
- Careers and work: skills, hiring, engineering culture, AI impact
- India Desk: relevant Indian technology developments, not the only focus

## Reader experience

The magazine must feel premium, creative, visual, and easy to use.

It should eventually support:
- Realistic page turns
- Desktop two-page spreads
- Mobile one-page reading
- Zoom and full screen
- Table of contents and page navigation
- Images, videos, GIFs, and interactive hotspots
- Image zoom, galleries, source links, polls, quizzes, sliders, and charts
- Visual page variety without losing brand consistency
- Responsive normal article pages for sharing and search

## Product principles

- Build an original product; do not copy another company’s code, branding, assets, or UI exactly.
- Keep a consistent brand system but allow controlled layout, theme, and animation variety.
- Make information clearer and more engaging, not merely more animated.
- Build reusable components and templates instead of rebuilding layouts every issue.
- Use AI as an assistant, not an autonomous publisher.
- Require human review before publishing.
- Prioritise reliable sources, original explanation, source transparency, asset rights, and corrections.

## Core technical principle

Code builds the reusable magazine machine.
Content data creates each issue.
AI edits content data, not the core template code.

## Long-term outcome

A repeatable editorial product where a human can curate and publish high-quality weekly or biweekly issues with AI assistance, eventually in roughly 5–10 focused hours per issue.
